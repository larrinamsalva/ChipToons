# Review a custom character's foot movement

In **Edit scene → Characters & movement**, select an animated puppet and open **Review Character Feet**. Choose left or right. The studio samples up to three seconds of that character's actual Stage motion starting near the current playhead. **Review From Current Frame** moves the inspection window; a suggested frame opens the existing Stage preview. Looking at the review alone does not rewrite the animation, source picture, project history or exported video.

The review works with separately parented vector or uploaded-picture foot parts outside the Workshop seating sequence. For each 24-fps sample, it projects the foot's sole point through its saved joints and actor transform. For an uploaded picture foot, open **Puppet Maker → Build a Character**, select the foot under **Body-part hierarchy**, assign its artwork and use **Mark the shoe sole** to move the yellow dot across and down the displayed picture. The mark is saved with that puppet; mirrored feet put it on the opposite side. **Reset sole to picture bottom** restores the default. Existing puppets without a mark still use the bottom center of their part rectangle. The original PNG, joint position and visible animation stay unchanged.

The review compares successive positions close to the rig's neutral-foot floor estimate and links up to five of the largest sideways intervals. The floor estimate moves with the character. It does not inspect transparent pixels, automatically find the real shoe sole, read scenery floor or camera depth, or detect collisions and physical balance. A mistaken mark can make the estimate inaccurate. A clean result is not proof of contact or natural motion.

For a painted foot moving sideways near that estimated floor, choose **Hold right/left Foot at Frame N** to move its measured sole horizontally back to the previous sample. This adds one editable foot joint key at that frame, at most 12 stage pixels, and saves through the normal project history. Undo restores the original. The correction requires an existing linear key interval with no timed gesture or talking cycle; it refuses an existing key at that time, automatic motion and unsupported rigs. It keeps the other foot, original endpoints and character path in place, but its interpolation changes the selected foot in nearby frames. Inspect the whole step and its scenery floor before export. It cannot guarantee balance, no clipping, or a sustained planted walk.

Choose **Hold right/left Foot for 3 Frames from Frame N** when the next two review samples are available and you want a brief planted pose. This inserts three editable foot joint keys as one Undo step, holding the original measured sole X at those three consecutive 24-fps samples. Each is bounded to 12 stage pixels from its uncorrected position; the tool refuses an authored key or unsupported motion anywhere in the span. The original endpoint keys, actor path, other foot and picture stay intact. The foot still blends into and out of that short hold, so preview the neighboring frames. It does not solve an entire walking cycle or detect collisions with the scenery.

The focused browser tests position and mirror the sole mark, save and reopen the cartoon, switch reviewed feet, jump to a real frame, apply one- and three-frame corrections, and verify backup and Undo/Redo. Short-media checks independently decode a genuinely sliding painted foot and corrected feet alongside their originals in exported WebMs, measuring painted pixels. This is an intentionally bad walk to verify measurement and local corrections, not a finished cartoon scene. The existing full-episode technical export remains a separate CI gate; a completed 15–20-minute children's episode still requires original art, voices, editing and artistic review.
