# Prepare a ChipToons cartoon for YouTube

1. Open **Finish My Cartoon → Prepare for YouTube**. If you already rendered a full movie in this session, the kit can use it. For a short cartoon, choose **Render YouTube WebM**. A larger layout starts at 1280 × 720 and can render at 800 × 450 instead. Phones use 800 × 450.
2. For a longer cartoon, export the numbered parts at matching settings. Open **Prepare My Complete Episode**, assign and verify every part, and choose **Make One Complete Movie**. From the verified movie, choose **Prepare for YouTube**. The kit uses the same continuous file and keeps its original resolution. The episode builder’s existing device, length, and storage limits apply.
3. Check the movie’s measured duration, resolution, and tracks. Use **Preview verified movie** to listen to the mix, then watch the complete downloaded file before uploading. If sound was expected and the track is missing, review the export and audio settings.
4. In **Thumbnail**, select a scene or enter the time of a real frame. Optionally move an existing character or artwork for the thumbnail only. Enter a short title, pick a readable color pair, and preview it at a small size. **Download thumbnail PNG** saves a 1280 × 720 image.
5. Review the title, description, learning topic, series, episode number, hashtags, and credits. Confirm the wording to download it. Where eligible, add real chapter timestamps and edit their labels. A new wording edit requires approval for that wording.
6. Download the verified WebM, thumbnail, SRT subtitles when available, video details, optional chapters, and publishing checklist individually. Keep the editable project backup separate. Draft wording and checkbox progress are kept while the finishing dialog is open. Download the text files to keep them.

## Little Fixers Club

Open **Turn My Picture Into a Cartoon → Use the Little Fixers Club poster → Open the animated workshop demo**. The 34-second cartoon has an animated host, a wobbling chair, narration, workshop audio, and timed captions. Choose a workshop frame for the thumbnail, review the details, and download the publishing files. The 20-minute storyboard is a separate editable plan.

## Video format and captions

ChipToons delivers WebM, a [supported YouTube upload format](https://support.google.com/youtube/troubleshooter/2888402). The kit reuses the existing recorder and local WebM assembler. MP4 conversion would require reliable H.264 and AAC support, muxing, and long-episode checks across supported devices. In the tested Chromium build, H.264 encoding was available, but AAC encoding and combined H.264/AAC MediaRecorder support were unavailable. This version offers verified WebM.

The kit shifts SRT cues by the recorder lead and uses the actual part starts for a joined movie. This keeps subtitle timing tied to the file rather than the planned storyboard duration. Direct kit renders keep subtitles separate. If a previous export drew captions into the frames, the kit reports that as well. The chapter list uses actual story boundaries mapped to the finished movie, with whole-second timestamps. [YouTube chapter requirements](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579)

## Manual publishing

Review artwork and music permissions, remove identifying information about children, show grown-up supervision for hazardous repairs, and check the complete movie and audio before upload. For Little Fixers episodes directed to ages 4–7, select **Yes, it’s Made for Kids** in YouTube Studio. Availability in the separate YouTube Kids app is determined by YouTube. Verify your account before uploading a video longer than 15 minutes. [Audience settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654) · [Longer uploads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/71673)

Preparation runs in the browser. Uploading the files and setting visibility are manual steps in YouTube Studio.
