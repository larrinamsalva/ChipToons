# Little Fixers Club — Mark It Safe (PR #36)

## Mission

Take the original Little Fixers story from **2:00 to 2:30** by teaching the first real repair-management skill: **identify a problem, stop using unsafe equipment, mark it out of use, and plan the adult inspection**. Do not mistake this for a finished chair repair.

This is a genuine **editable original 2.5D/vector cartoon**, not animated photos, slide loops or native 3D cinema. The longer 20-minute media regression uses unrelated fixture footage, **not** a finished Little Fixers episode.

## Watch and act

On the no-login dashboard select **Watch Scout mark it safe (2:30)**. This creates a new project derived from the existing 2-minute Helper Jo demo, preserving all four previously built scenes and three approved original Scout voice takes. Previous demo buttons remain unchanged.

### New scene: 2:00–2:30

1. **Notice a problem**: Scout spots the still-unusable chair and points toward the issue while keeping a safe distance.
2. **Stop using it**: Scout takes a small step back; Jo acknowledges the report.
3. **Mark it out of use**: Helper Jo—an independent full-body vector puppet—takes a warning sign into the puppet hand, carries it and places it on the FLOOR beside the chair, not on the furniture. The prop moves with the hand and releases in an actual `put` interaction; timed visual labels clearly communicate **OUT OF USE**.
4. **Plan with a grown-up**: Jo points to the labelled, off-limits chair. No one sits on it, shakes it, repairs it, or uses dangerous tools; any inspection comes after the child is safely away.

This 30-second scene includes independently authored Scout and Jo body, arm, leg, head and emotion keyframes, a real hand-attached warning sign, three camera shots, four educational labels, scene ambience, paper/placement cues and reassuring chimes.
