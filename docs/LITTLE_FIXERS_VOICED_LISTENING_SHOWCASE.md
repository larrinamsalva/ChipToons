# PR #101 — Little Fixers Original-Voice Listening Showcase

## New public, no-login example
From the home dashboard choose **Watch Helper Jo listen to Scout (0:34)**.
This opens an actual editable 34-second cartoon called
**Little Fixers Club — Scout Speaks, Helper Jo Listens**.

The scene features two separately rigged original workshop vector
performers. Scout walks and speaks three previously approved,
original recorded Little Fixers voice lines. Helper Jo is silent:
Jo looks toward Scout during Scout's genuine recorded dialogue and
settles into natural expression during pauses.

## Real source and preservation
- Directly constructs a NEW project from the existing
  littleFixersScoutVoiced demo. All three original recorded media
  sources, original approved clips, scripted exact wording,
  actual waveform-derived saved mouth tracks and captions remain.
- Adds a separate original **Helper Jo** puppet and three editable,
  expression-only mood keyframes. There is NO fabricated Jo voice,
  unapproved transcript, generated media or dubbed line.
- The project enables **Cinematic Storybook**, so the already-merged
  PR #100 voice-driven listening gaze/head behavior appears in a
  playable example instead of only in unit fixtures.
- The old single-performer voiced example, 30-second articulated
  handoff, long Little Fixers story and twenty-minute delivery
  fixture are not changed. No schema or audio editing.
- Play, pause, seek, backup and genuine WebM all use the same
  Stage frames, and the normal no-login home entry point.
- A browser test opens the public demo and checks saved project
  integrity, real approved audio, two independent rigged actors,
  and visible conversation cues during actual voiced speech.
  The original voiced Scout regression stays green as well.

## Limits
This showcases one voiced actor and one silent listener. It does
not claim two newly recorded character voices, automatic dialogue
generation, multi-actor voice casting or a full 3D cinematic engine.
