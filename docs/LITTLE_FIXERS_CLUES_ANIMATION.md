## Safety and creative intent

Children learn to **look, notice and describe clues**, not to touch or attempt to repair an unstable chair. No child is shown using tools or testing the chair. A trusted adult must decide whether any repair is safe. The small chair movement is an animation illustrating instability, not a real structural diagnosis. The circle shows a *possible* gap; it does not claim an actual repair has occurred.

Reuse the poster's existing chair and host art. All workshop sounds already exist in the project's previous audio library. No extra audio asset is added, avoiding the 24-asset limit.
