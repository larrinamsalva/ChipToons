## Priority order and acceptance evidence

1. **Ship a genuinely finished pilot and then a full episode.** Record/approve original dialogue and sound, finish distinctive shots and movement for every scene, inspect decoded frames and listen across the complete movie. Report actual runtime, voices, unique visuals and artistic review separately from the engineering fixture.
2. **Make photo-based characters easier to animate.** #182 reuses a visible traced limb across the body; #183 gives traced elbow/knee/wrist pieces their own parented joints; #184 keeps the original resting picture still when its joint parent is changed. Continue with consistent reusable looks and editable missing/occluded details. Verify saved character references and exported scenes after reuse. Automatic arbitrary-photo conversion or generated anime/3D looks would require a separately designed model/service or a constrained local pipeline; do not imply it is in the current app.
3. **Improve animation across custom rigs.** #185 exposes a bounded read-only picture/vector foot travel check in ordinary scenes; #186 adds a manually positioned picture-foot sole mark; #187–#189 add short picture-foot holds; #190 extends these to vector boots. Next, support more expressive motion without foot sliding, plus clipping/balance diagnostics using sampled Stage frames; verify across walking, sitting, dancing and object interaction in decoded output. Preserve authored keyframes and source art.
4. **Raise output quality within real device limits.** #177 repaired the dropped-frame 1080p path with independent decoded-file proof. Next, measure performance on real devices, ensure practical storage/part joining for a full 1080p episode, and consider MP4 only after browser capability and decoded-file proof. Do not label a short 1080p technical test a complete 1080p episode.
5. **Validate the nontechnical workflow.** Test a real creator starting from a picture, making characters/scenes/voices and recovering a project, then exporting on Windows/Android/iOS where supported. Record blocked actions and fix them with focused PRs; keep the public demo free of paid requirements.

Every future feature PR should preserve the current project and media formats where possible, include meaningful behavior tests, and use independent media decoding when picture or sound output changes. Keep PRs separate, repair failed checks on their own branches, and leave merging to the owner.
