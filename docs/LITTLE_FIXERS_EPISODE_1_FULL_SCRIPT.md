# Little Fixers Club — Episode 1: Fix It, Don’t Toss It!

## Full episode script and production map (draft; not yet an animated movie)

**Source:** ChipToons PR #25 provides an independently verified 1:59 animated, voiced opening. This document provides the remaining 18:01 of original dialogue, scene direction, and creative prompts. Together the timecodes target a 20:00 story.

**Important:** These are planned time slots, not proven speech or video lengths. The closing time must be adjusted after recording, editing, and genuine decoding. Do not claim the episode is finished until each scene has distinct animation, approved voice audio, captions, and a verified rendered movie.

**Audience:** ages 4–7. **Tone:** welcoming, inclusive, playful and calm. **Core lesson:** notice a problem, ask a trusted adult, repair or reuse only when safe, and understand when to stop.

**Cast:** Workshop Host (existing rig from original poster); three children from original reference art (new independent cutouts/rigs are still required); trusted adult helper (new art required), dog and cat as safely placed background/reaction characters. These are production roles, not claims that these characters have been animated. Children’s names may be chosen later by the creator.

**Safety:** no unsupervised repair or powered tool use by young children; a grown-up decides furniture safety, all fan inspection is adult-only with fan unplugged, and any birdhouse wood cutting/drilling/fastening is adult-only. Illustrations should model safety without giving hazardous step-by-step tool instructions.
