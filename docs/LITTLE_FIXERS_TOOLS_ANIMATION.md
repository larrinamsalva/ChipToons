## Safety and story direction

The host models respect for tools and how to be a helpful member of the team without touching dangerous equipment. Adults control any sharp, hot, electrical or repair equipment. The child-safe action is to create a checklist and **count soft cloth, parts tray and checklist**. These three supplies are text callouts, not a claim that we added new physically illustrated 3D props. The new original moving checklist is a book prop and the original toolbox remains stored.
