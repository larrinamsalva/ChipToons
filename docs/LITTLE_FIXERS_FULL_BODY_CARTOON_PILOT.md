# Little Fixers Club — Original Full-Body Cartoon Acting Pilot

**Purpose:** answer the critical creative question: *Are we making an original animated cartoon, or moving pieces of the uploaded poster?* This is a separate, **34-second original cartoon animation pilot** built independently from the uploaded sketch. It does **not** modify the original photo-cutout Little Fixers episode, and does not claim that the 20-minute episode is finished.

## How to open

From the ChipToons home dashboard, select **Try the full-body cartoon workshop**. No login, picture upload, image tracing, or poster is required. The project opens editable in the existing creator and can be backed up/exported like other demonstrations.

## What is actually new

- A **full-body**, procedurally illustrated 2D vector character named *Scout* with separate head, arms, torso, legs, black work boots, cargo-style pockets and toolbelt.
- A **true animated walk cycle**: the body's horizontal position advances while left/right legs and opposite arms alternate; the host comes to a full stop, turns their head, points, and reacts.
- Authentic eye blinking and subtle idle breathing.
- A small original 2.5D workshop set with shelves, a workbench, a stored toolbox, book, wagon and recycling bin in **foreground and background layers**. A gentle camera follow, a safe-tools close-up and a return shot create visual depth.
- Room tone, synchronized little footsteps and a success cue. All art is generated by existing vector drawing primitives; no borrowed image assets or user-specific photo is necessary.
- A short original, **unrecorded** educational script and teaching labels. This pilot is not voiced and does not have approved lip sync or speech captions.

## Scope and limitations

This is **not a true 3D mesh character or a cinematic rendered-film-quality cartoon**. It is a working, editable **2.5D vector animation prototype** built with the existing SVG stage and puppet joints. Its wardrobe uses specific illustrated forms, not a photorealistic texture. It does not replace the user's actual characters or source art and is not part of the 20-minute episode timeline yet.

The original picture-to-cartoon pipeline, independent 1:59 voiced opening, Safety First, Meet the Tools, Look for Clues draft PR and all full-length storyboard plans remain unchanged.

## How we verify this is genuinely animated

- Browser checks inspect the **nine distinct rig parts**, their identity and costume, twelve distinct animation keyframes, independent changing leg angles, a large change in stage position, the three camera shots, visible scene depth and three working effects.
- The real-video test renders 34 seconds of WebM and independently checks movement between decoded frames before claiming that the short export is valid. The project contains no pasted poster or picture reference.
- Audio exists only for sound effects. Two script lines remain correctly marked unrecorded.

## Production next steps

Once the original vector character/acting pilot is visually approved, build an intentional series cast and world style guide, including matching outfits, expressive face poses, repeatable walk/look/point/turn animations, coordinated camera blocking, and voice actor/mouth synchronization. Then integrate the approved character style into the running Episode 1 project in a **separate, reviewed** PR. Do not replace the existing 1:59 voiced opening or draft scenes without an explicit migration plan and visual comparison.
